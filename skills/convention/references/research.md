# Research and evidence

- This function does not select a role or execution route. Use the current assignment and authority.
- Apply only the guidance needed by the investigation; reuse supplied evidence and instructions.

<a id="research"></a>

## 1. Research and evidence

- Investigate web, code, Documents and data within the question, scope, constraints and completion criteria.
- Preserve sources, locations, collection context, fidelity and limitations. Distinguish observations,
  analysis, hypotheses, conclusions, contradictions and gaps; findings do not change accepted Specifications.
- Verify distribution, ratio and primary-cause claims against available data before recommending action.
  If blocked, label estimates, their basis and how to verify them.
- Use [Agent](../../agent/SKILL.md) for execution authority and [Testing](testing.md#agent-graph-boundary)
  for checks. Durable evidence follows [Document](../../document/SKILL.md); temporary material stays in its task/run.
